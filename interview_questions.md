# Task 2 – Interview Questions

### 1. What is the purpose of EDA?
Exploratory Data Analysis is used to understand the structure, quality, distributions, relationships, and unusual observations in a dataset before statistical modeling or machine learning.

### 2. How do boxplots help in understanding a dataset?
A boxplot summarizes the median, quartiles, interquartile range, and potential outliers. It helps compare spread and identify observations that may require investigation.

### 3. What is correlation and why is it useful?
Correlation measures the strength and direction of association between two variables. It helps identify relationships and potentially redundant predictors.

### 4. How do you detect skewness in data?
Use a skewness statistic and inspect a histogram/density plot. A long tail toward one side indicates an asymmetric distribution.

### 5. What is multicollinearity?
Multicollinearity occurs when predictor variables are highly correlated with one another. It can make coefficient-based models unstable and make individual feature effects difficult to interpret.

### 6. What tools do you use for EDA?
Pandas and NumPy for data manipulation/statistics; Matplotlib and Seaborn for visualizations; Plotly for interactive charts; Jupyter Notebook or VS Code for analysis.

### 7. Can you explain a time when EDA helped you find a problem?
A practical example is identifying missing values, duplicates, unexpected ranges, or extreme observations during initial inspection. Finding these issues before modeling prevents them from silently affecting results.

### 8. What is the role of visualization in ML?
Visualization makes distributions, relationships, anomalies, trends, and class separation easier to understand. It supports preprocessing, feature selection, model diagnostics, and communication.
